[Opening Chorus]

Quietly I let this song begin
A tiny spark beneath my skin
I breathe it out into the night
And watch it grow into the light

So let it shine across the sky
Until the dark begins to cry
Even if the stars are far away
I’ll keep this flame alive today

[Verse 1]

I used to hide the words I knew
Behind the rain, behind the blue
Afraid that if I let them go
The world would never hear them glow

But in the silence, soft and deep
A dream was waking from its sleep
It touched my heart, it called my name
And nothing ever felt the same

[Pre-Chorus]

Now every step I take is bright
A silver line through endless night
If I’m afraid, I’ll sing it through
This little spark will lead me to you

[Chorus]

So let it shine across the sky
A melody that learns to fly
No matter how the shadows fall
This voice will rise above it all

Let it shine into the dawn
Even while the night goes on
Every echo finds a way
To reach another heart someday


[Verse 2 - enter immediately, same groove]

Far past the noise, through crowded air

A single note, a sudden turn

So I will send these words along
No longer scared they don’t belong
And if they tremble as they start
They’ll find a home in someone’s heart

[Pre-Chorus 2]

Now every breath becomes a sign

This little spark will cross the night

[Chorus 2]

So let it shine across the sky
A melody that learns to fly
No matter where the shadows fall
We’ll raise our voices through it all

Let it shine beyond the dawn
A quiet strength to carry on
Even when the seasons turn
The light we share will always burn

[Bridge]

It started smaller than a sigh
Too soft to reach the open sky
But every time I let it free
It sounded more and more like me

No thunder called, no mountains moved
Just one small note I knew was true
And in the dark, a voice replied
Another spark lit up the night

[Final Chorus]

So let it shine across the sky
A thousand songs begin to fly
No matter how the shadows fall
Our voices rise above it all

Let it shine beyond the dawn
The light we made will carry on
